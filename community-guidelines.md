# Community Guidelines

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

spottr is for finding workout partners — people to train with, push you, and make the gym better. To keep it safe and welcoming for everyone, every member agrees to these guidelines. They work alongside our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service).

**We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior.**

---

## Be respectful

- Treat others the way you'd want to be treated at your gym.
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, bullying, or intimidation.
- No unwanted sexual advances, sexual content, or solicitation. spottr is not a hookup app.

## Be real

- Be yourself. No impersonation, fake profiles, or misrepresenting your age.
- You must be **18 or older** to use spottr.

## Keep it safe

- Meet in your gym during staffed or busy hours, especially the first time.
- Tell a friend where you're going.
- Don't share others' personal information, and protect your own.
- Trust your instincts — if something feels off, leave.

## Keep it clean

- No spam, advertising, solicitation, or scams.
- No illegal content or activity.
- Don't share or solicit sexual content, and never anything involving minors — we report this to authorities.

---

## Reporting and blocking

You're in control of who you interact with:

- **Block** anyone to stop them from seeing or contacting you.
- **Report** anyone who breaks these guidelines — in the App, or by emailing **support@spottr-io.com**.
- **Ghost mode** lets you go fully invisible at any time.

We review reports of objectionable content and abusive users and aim to **act within 24 hours**. Action may include removing content and removing the user from spottr.

---

## Consequences

Breaking these guidelines can lead to content removal, suspension, or permanent removal from spottr — at our discretion, with or without notice.

Thanks for helping keep spottr a great place to train. 💪
