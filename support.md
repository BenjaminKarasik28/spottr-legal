# Support

We read every message.

## Contact us

- **In the app:** go to **Settings → Help / Contact us**. This is fastest, because your account details come with your message.
- **By email:** [support@spottr-io.com](mailto:support@spottr-io.com). Use this if you can't sign in.

We reply to the email address on your account.

## Safety

<div class="callout"><strong>If you're in immediate danger, call 911</strong> or your local emergency number.</div>

To report a member, open their profile and tap **Report**. You can also tap **Block**. Once you block someone, you won't see each other anywhere on spottr, and they aren't notified. We review every report within 24 hours. See our [Community Guidelines](community-guidelines).

## Common questions

**Why don't I see any matches?**
Matches are members who share one of your venues. Add your gym in the app, set your workout schedule, and make sure **Invisible mode** is off in Settings.

**Who can see my profile?**
Only members who share a venue with you. They see your **first name only**, never your last name. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).

**How do I hide my profile?**
Turn on **Invisible mode** in Settings. You won't appear in anyone's matches, and you can't browse or chat until you turn it off.

**I blocked someone by mistake.**
Go to **Settings → Blocked members** and tap **Unblock**. Your previous connection isn't restored, so you'd need to reconnect.

**I can't sign in.**
Make sure you're using the same method you signed up with: email, Google, or Apple. If you're still stuck, email [support@spottr-io.com](mailto:support@spottr-io.com) from the address on your account.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Settings → Delete account** in the app. Don't have the app? See [Delete your account](delete-account).
