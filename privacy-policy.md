# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how **[OPERATOR]** ("spottr," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the **spottr** mobile application (the "App"). spottr helps people find workout partners at gyms and other fitness venues they share.

**spottr is for adults aged 18 and over only.** We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### a. Information you give us

- **Account details:** first name, last name, optional middle name, and email address.
- **Profile details:** city, state, ZIP/postal code, gender, age, a short "fitness label," and an optional profile photo.
- **Workout preferences:** the days and times you train, your workout types, your skill level per venue, and your preferred partner age range.
- **Prompt answers:** free-text answers to profile prompts you choose to fill in.
- **Venues:** the gyms and fitness venues you join, and any new venue you submit for us to add.
- **Messages:** the content of messages you send to other members through the App.
- **Reports and support requests:** information you provide when you report another user or contact us.

### b. Information collected automatically

- **Precise location (GPS):** only when you use the "add a venue" feature and grant permission. We use it to find fitness venues near you. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings; the feature simply won't sort by proximity without it.
- **Push notification token:** a device identifier used to deliver notifications (e.g., new friend requests and messages). You control notifications in your device settings.
- **Technical and log data:** standard information our hosting providers process to run and secure the service, such as IP address, request timestamps, and error logs.

### c. Information from sign-in providers

If you sign in with **Apple** or **Google**, we receive your name and email address from that provider to create your account. Apple may provide a private relay email instead of your real address; that's fine — we use whatever the provider sends.

We do **not** collect government IDs, payment card numbers, precise health/medical records, or biometric data.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- **Create and run your account** and authenticate you securely.
- **Provide matching:** show you potential workout partners who share a venue with you, ranked using your stated schedule, workout types, skill level, age, and preferences — and show your profile to others on the same basis.
- **Enable connections and chat:** friend requests, friendships, and messaging.
- **Send notifications** you've enabled (requests, messages).
- **Keep the community safe:** review reports, prevent abuse and spam, and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.
- **Operate, maintain, and improve** the App.
- **Comply with law** and protect the rights, property, and safety of users and the public.

**Legal bases (EEA/UK users):** performance of our contract with you (providing the App); your consent (precise location, push notifications); our legitimate interests (safety, abuse prevention, improving the service); and compliance with legal obligations.

---

## 3. What other users can see

spottr is a social app, so parts of your profile are visible to other members **who share a venue with you**:

- Your **first name** (we never show your last name to other members), age, gender, city/state, fitness label, profile photo, skill level, bio prompt answers, and the venues you have in common.

Please keep these points in mind:

- **Profile photos** are stored privately and shown to other members through temporary, expiring links that require an active, signed-in session — they are not placed at permanently public URLs. Still, anyone who shares a venue with you can view your photo in the App, so don't upload anything you wouldn't want a potential workout partner to see.
- **Messages** are visible to the people in that conversation.
- **Ghost mode** makes you invisible — when enabled, you're excluded from matches in both directions and others can't see or message you.

## 4. How we share information

We share information only as described here. **We do not sell your personal information.**

- **Service providers (processors)** who run the App on our behalf:
  - **Supabase** — authentication and storage of profile photos.
  - **Railway** — hosting of our database and API.
  - **Expo** — push notification delivery and app build/distribution.
  - **Apple** and **Google** — sign-in and app distribution through their app stores.
- **Other users** — the profile information described in Section 3.
- **Legal and safety** — to law enforcement or others when required by law, or when we believe in good faith it's necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of any person.
- **Business transfers** — if spottr is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may transfer as part of that deal.

---

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- We keep your information while your account is active.
- You can **delete your account at any time from within the App**. Deleting your account removes your login identity and your app data — your profile, schedule, preferences, bio, gym memberships, friend requests, friendships, passes, and the conversations and messages you are part of. Venues you created are kept (others may rely on them) but are no longer linked to you.
- Residual copies may persist briefly in encrypted backups and in our providers' logs before being overwritten in the ordinary course.

---

## 6. Security

We protect your information with measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated API access using short-lived tokens, and administrative keys held only on our servers (never in the App). No system is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and to address issues promptly.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

- **Access and update** your profile information in the App.
- **Delete** your account and data from the App.
- **Control location** and **notification** permissions in your device settings.
- **Use ghost mode** to become invisible.
- **Block or report** other members.

**EEA/UK residents** also have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data; to object to processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to request access and deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. We do **not** sell or "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The categories we collect are described in Section 1 (identifiers, profile characteristics such as age/gender, geolocation, photos, user content, and usage/technical data).

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app controls or email **support@spottr-io.com**. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
