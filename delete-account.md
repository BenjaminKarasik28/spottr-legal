# Delete your spottr account

This page explains how to delete your **spottr** account and the data that goes with it.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open spottr and go to **Profile → Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

## Without the app

Email **[support@spottr-io.com](mailto:support@spottr-io.com?subject=Delete%20my%20account)** from the email address on your account, with the subject **"Delete my account."** We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

## What gets deleted

- Your login (email, Google, or Apple sign-in)
- Your profile: name, age, gender, location, fitness label, and photo
- Your venues, workout schedule, preferences, and bio answers
- Your friend requests, connections, and passes
- Your conversations and messages
- Contact-form messages stored with your account

## What we keep

- **Reports.** We keep reports you filed, and reports other members filed about you, after an account is deleted. This lets us investigate abuse, and it means nobody can erase their history by deleting their account.
- **Venues you added** stay listed, since others may use them, but they're no longer linked to you.
- **Support emails** already in our inbox are kept as correspondence.
- **Backups.** Leftover copies may stay briefly in encrypted backups and our providers' logs until they're overwritten in the normal course.
